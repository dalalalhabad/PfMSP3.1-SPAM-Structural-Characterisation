## Analysis principle

The AF3 models will be compared directly with the original experimental NMR
observations/restraints reported for SPAM-H1.

The deposited 1PSM coordinate ensemble will not be treated as the primary
experimental benchmark because those coordinates represent an interpretation
of the experimental NMR data.

Structure-to-structure comparison with 1PSM may be retained as supplementary
context only.
