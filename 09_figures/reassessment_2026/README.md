# Structural reassessment figures — 2026

This directory contains figures generated during the 2026 structural
reassessment of the historical PfMSP3.1 SPAM model.

These figures are separate from the figures recovered from the original
2020 Master's thesis.

## Figures

### `Experimental_NMR_distance-restraint.png`

Visual summary associated with evaluation of the experimental NMR
distance-restraint evidence used in the sequence-matched structural
reassessment.

### `Local_Cα_RMSD_between_the_historical_MODELLER.png`

Local Cα RMSD comparison between the historical MODELLER structure and
the sequence-matched AlphaFold 3 predictions.

The local comparison focuses on structurally relevant regions rather
than relying solely on full-length RMSD, which is strongly influenced by
regions outside the experimentally constrained SPAM-H1 segment.
