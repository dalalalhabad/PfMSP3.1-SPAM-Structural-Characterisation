# Figures

This directory preserves figures associated with both the original 2020
Master's project and the subsequent 2026 structural reassessment of the
PfMSP3.1 SPAM domain.

Figures are separated according to their provenance.

## Historical 2020 figures

Historical thesis figures are stored under:

`historical_2020/thesis_figures/`

These figures originate from the original Master's research project and
include material associated with:

- sequence analysis;
- structural modelling;
- molecular dynamics;
- salt-bridge analysis;
- protein–protein docking; and
- the overall historical computational workflow.

A detailed inventory of the recovered historical figures is available at:

`../docs/figure_inventory.md`

The historical figure collection is preserved as research provenance and
should not be interpreted as having been newly generated in 2026.

## 2026 reassessment figures

Figures generated during the subsequent structural reassessment are
stored separately under:

`reassessment_2026/`

These figures document analyses performed during the modern
sequence-matched reassessment and are not part of the original 2020
Master's thesis figure set.

Current reassessment figures include:

- `Experimental_NMR_distance-restraint.png`
- `Local_Cα_RMSD_between_the_historical_MODELLER.png`

The corresponding numerical analyses, scripts and derived results are
documented under:

`../04_AF3_vs_experimental_NMR/`

## Provenance

The separation between `historical_2020/` and `reassessment_2026/` is
intentional.

It distinguishes figures originating from the original Master's project
from figures generated during later reassessment and repository
reconstruction work.
